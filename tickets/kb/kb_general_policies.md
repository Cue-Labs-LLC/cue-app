# Familiar Faces — General Policies & Terms

## About Familiar Faces

Familiar Faces is a live events company that throws afrobeats, dancehall, and R&B parties celebrating Black culture and community. We host events across multiple cities including Los Angeles, Las Vegas, Phoenix, New York City, San Francisco, San Diego, and Seattle.

- Website: www.familiarfaces.la
- Contact email: info@familiarfaces.la

## Ticket & Refund Policy

- All ticket sales are final.
- No refunds or exchanges for any reason, including illness, travel complications, personal emergencies, or changes in plans.

- A ticket is a revocable license to attend the specific event listed and is subject to the Terms of Service and venue rules.
- Familiar Faces reserves the right to refuse or revoke admission at its discretion.

- By purchasing a ticket, attendees grant consent to event organizers and designated personnel to photograph or record video during the event.
- Photos and videos may be used for promotional, marketing, or archival purposes.
- If an attendee prefers not to be photographed or recorded, they should notify event staff. The team will do its best to accommodate, though exclusion cannot be guaranteed due to the dynamic nature of live events.
- Attendees sharing their own photos or videos are asked to respect others' privacy and obtain consent when appropriate.

### Common questions about photo/video

Q: Will there be photographers at the event?
A: Yes. By purchasing a ticket, you consent to being photographed or recorded. The content may be used for promotional purposes.

Q: I don't want to be in any photos. What do I do?
