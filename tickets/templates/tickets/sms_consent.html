01

The Cue messaging program

Cue Labs LLC operates a customer analytics and event ticketing platform at cueup.co. The Cue SMS program sends text messages to attendees who have purchased tickets through Cue and to account holders who have created a Cue account.

Messages are sent from a Cue-operated toll-free phone number on behalf of Cue and the event organizers that Cue customers have purchased tickets from. Messages fall into two categories:

  • Transactional. Order confirmations, event reminders, schedule or venue changes, ticket transfer notifications, and login verification codes.
  • Promotional. Announcements about upcoming events from organizers the recipient has previously purchased from, and occasional product updates from Cue.

02

How recipients opt in

Cue collects SMS consent in two places. In both cases, the consent checkbox is unchecked by default and the recipient must affirmatively check it. Consent is not a condition of purchase or of creating an account.

1. At ticket checkout. When buying tickets through Cue, the checkout page shows this opt-in:

Checkout page
Yes, I'd like to receive promotional texts from [Event Organizer Name] about upcoming events and ticket offers. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

2. At account creation. When a user creates a Cue account and provides a phone number, the profile-completion screen shows this opt-in:

Account signup
By checking this box, you authorize Cue and event organizers you register with to send you automated informational and marketing texts. Your consent is not a condition of any purchase. Messages may be sent any time of day and message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Phone numbers are not shared, sold, or rented to third parties. SMS consent is stored separately from other marketing preferences and is honored independently — opting out of SMS does not opt you out of email, and vice versa.

04

Message frequency & fees

Frequency. Message frequency varies based on event activity and the organizers a recipient has purchased from. A typical subscriber receives 2–6 messages per month. Around an event they have tickets to, frequency may briefly increase (e.g., the day-before reminder and a day-of reminder).

Cost. Cue does not charge for SMS messages. However, message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier and plan. Cue is not responsible for carrier fees.

Carrier support. Cue's messaging program is supported on all major US carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, US Cellular) and most regional carriers.

05

Keywords & commands

Reply to any Cue message with one of the following keywords to control your subscription:

Keyword What happens
STOP You will be unsubscribed from all Cue marketing messages. We will send one final confirmation message and then nothing further. STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, and QUIT behave the same way.
HELP You will receive a message with our support email and a reminder of how to opt out. INFO behaves the same way.
START If you previously opted out, you will be resubscribed. UNSTOP and YES behave the same way.

Standard responses recipients receive:

  • After STOP: "You have been unsubscribed from Cue messages. No more messages will be sent. Reply START to resubscribe."
  • After HELP: "Cue: For help, contact support@cueup.co. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe."
  • After START: "You are resubscribed to Cue messages. Msg frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

06

Carrier disclaimer

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Cue uses Twilio as our messaging provider and a registered toll-free number that complies with carrier requirements for transactional and promotional messaging.

Delivery to ported numbers, prepaid plans, and certain international carriers may be limited.

07

Privacy

Phone numbers and SMS consent records are stored according to our privacy policy. We do not share, sell, or rent phone numbers to third parties for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are used only to deliver the messages described above.

If you delete your Cue account, your phone number and SMS consent record are permanently removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
