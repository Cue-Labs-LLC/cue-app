Returning audience: {{ ticket_noun }} bought by people who already came to an earlier show in that city, our best proxy for built-up organic demand (some may have clicked an ad). New buyers / $1k: {{ ticket_noun }} from first-timers in that city per $1,000 of ad spend, i.e. how efficiently ads grow the market. Fill rate: paid tickets as a share of each event's capacity (or its venue's), averaged across events. Always paid only, since most free RSVPs don't show up. 1 wk out: the same, counting only paid tickets ordered by a week before the show.
