Getting an SMS-eligible audience

You can only text customers who have opted in to marketing SMS. The SMS Subscriber column shows who's eligible (green check), and you can filter by SMS status using the funnel on that column. There are three ways to grow this list:

  • On import — map a consent column from your CSV (Settings → CSV Formats → "SMS Marketing Consent").
  • Going forward — collect opt-ins at ticket purchase or via opt-in forms.
  • Manually — select customers you already have consent from, then SMS status → Opt in to SMS in the actions menu.

Only opt in customers who have genuinely consented to marketing texts — sending to non-consented contacts violates carrier and TCPA rules.
